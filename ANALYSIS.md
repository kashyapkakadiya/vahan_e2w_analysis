## Top-performing brands

* **TVS Motor was #1 in all ten months, with Bajaj Auto #2, Ather Energy #3 and Hero MotoCorp (Vida) #4.**
* **#5 was Ola Electric in every month except February**, when Greaves (Ampere) outsold it (4,762 vs 4,177).

## Key trends and share changes (January → September, the last full month)

1. **The market grew about 61%** (129k → 209k) with strong seasonality: a sharp March spike (+68.5%, financial year-end), an April pullback (−20.9%), a dip in August (−10.2%) and a September rebound (+12.9%).
2. **Bajaj gained the most share (+2.8 pp, 20.6% → 23.4%).** Its units grew 83%, and September (48,740) was its best month so far, just ahead of March.
3. **Ather lost the most share (−3.3 pp, 18.2% → 14.8%).** Its volume is flat at about 29–31k a month since March, so it grew 32% against the market's 61%.
4. **TVS stayed on top but with less share (−1.9 pp).** It fell to a low of 24.3% in June, then recovered to 27.2% in provisional October (58,428 units, its highest so far).
5. **Hero Vida is gaining** (+1.0 pp by September), and provisional October shows 28,745 units and 13.4% share, its highest. That figure is still subject to back-fill.
6. **Ola is volatile.** Registrations fell 46.5% in February (3.5% share), peaked at 16,296 in June (8.3%; share peaked at 8.9% in May), then declined every month to 11,614 in provisional October (5.4%).
7. **Smaller makers grew faster than the market.** River Mobility (+98%) and BGauss (+98%) nearly doubled between January and September, and Simple Energy grew 144% from a small base (0.4% → 0.7%). E-Sprinto is erratic (120 units in August, about 2,000 in September).
8. **Concentration is easing slightly.** The top-3 share fell from 66.7% to 64.2% (63.1% in provisional October), while the top 5 stay at about 82–84%.

## Assumptions, limitations and data gaps

* **Partial October.** The export was taken on 31 Oct 2026, so October is month-to-date. Treat its MoM and shares as provisional.
* **Back-filling.** Vahan adds registrations after the fact, so earlier months may also have changed since extraction, and a later download will not match exactly.
* **Registrations, not sales.** Vahan counts retail registrations, which differ from company-reported wholesale or dispatch figures.
* **No total row.** The export has none, so the industry total is the sum of all makers. Each maker's TOTAL column was checked against the sum of its months (no mismatches).
* **Brands are Vahan maker entities.** I shortened some names and merged two Kinetic Green entries. Importer-led entries (for example "ZAP (IMPORTER: …)") remain separate. Hero MotoCorp (Vida) and Hero Electric are different companies, and Ampere Vehicles Pvt Ltd (27 units) is kept separate from Greaves.
* **"OTHERS".** Vahan's own catch-all maker (3,244 units, mostly in January–March) counts in the totals but is never ranked.
* **MoM on small bases.** Growth is blank where the previous month was zero, and the workbook flags a low base (previous month below 100 units). Very large percentages, such as E-Sprinto's +1,564% in September, are not meaningful.
* **Filter scope.** Results reflect only the vehicle classes, fuels and "All India" setting listed above. States or RTOs not yet on the dashboard may not be included in "All India", and I could not check this from the file.
